App Info (Read Me)

🧠 Adrena-AI
  • Use this AI for explanations, note-making, personal assistance, and more. Please use appropriate language—unreasonable behavior may lead to temporary blocking.

  • Note: Some responses may be inaccurate. But guess what? The full version is loading...
  • Donate Past Questions (PQ) for any university, UTME/WAEC, or School of Nursing to earn rewards.
🔐 How to Register, Verify Account, Login, and Reset Password
  • Click the Register button on the welcome page.
  • Provide your username, email, and password.
  • Verify your email using the OTP sent to your inbox (check your spam folder if you don’t see it).
  • Login using your verified email and password.
  • If you forget your password, use the Reset Password option on the login page.
  • Refresh the page if you encounter "INTERNAL SERVER ERROR", it's just a temporary issue, a simple refresh resolves that.
💳 The Dashboard
  • After logging in, go to your dashboard.
  • Click GENERATE PIN, choose your preferred payment method (Paystack, direct bank transfer, or other options by contacting the admin).
  • For Paystack, tick the checkbox to proceed. Pay the exact amount shown; your PIN will be emailed to you automatically.
  • For bank transfer, carefully copy the account details, pay the exact amount, and send payment proof to the admin (Recommended).
  • Return and click ENTER PIN to input your generated PIN.
  • Join our groups from the dashboard for admission updates, tutorials, and mentorship.
📚 Enter PIN & Exam Page
  • Enter your PIN carefully to access the exam. After 5 incorrect attempts, access will be blocked for 24 hours. Note: The PIN is device-bound—contact admin if your device changes.
  • You can switch between Light/Dark mode using the toggle button.
  • All subjects are available, they include: English, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Current Affairs, Accounting, Commerce, Art subjects & more...
  • English is automatically selected, you will need to select 3 more subjects and then click the Start Exam button.
  • Always submit your exam before exiting the page. Submitting more than once will erase your answers and explanations.
  • Use the Retake Last Exam button to retry and track improvement. (Make sure to submit the previous exam first!)
  • You can view your last and past results, including rankings on the leaderboard.
  • Awards will be given to top performers on the leaderboard.
🕒 Time & Grading
  • The exam lasts 2 hrs.
  • Scores are calculated per selected subjects(100 for each). Percentages are also shown.
  • Check your results, performance chart, and explanations. You can retake the same questions to monitor improvement.
  • The leaderboard shows top scorers from fresh exams (retakes are excluded).
  • Awards will be given to top students on the leaderboard.
🔄 Please update the app every 2 weeks for new questions and features.
