JAMB CBT Instructions

Kindly adhere to the following instructions carefully

  • Candidates must have registered for the JAMB examination and possess a valid JAMB registration number.
  • The JAMB exam is a Computer-Based Test (CBT) consisting of four subjects — English Language (compulsory) and three others related to your course.
  • Each subject contains 40 questions except English with 60 questions totaling 180 questions to be answered within 2 hours (120 minutes).
  • All questions carry equal marks. There is no negative marking.
  • Candidates are required to read each question carefully before selecting an answer.
  • You must not communicate with other candidates during the examination.
  • Mobile phones, calculators, watches, and electronic devices are strictly prohibited in the exam hall.
  • Biometric verification will be conducted before entering the exam hall — ensure your fingerprints are properly captured.
  • Arrive at the CBT center at least 30 minutes before your scheduled exam time.
  • Dressing must be smart and decent; improper dressing may lead to disqualification.
JAMB scores are calculated over 400 marks. Practice consistently and manage your time wisely to improve your performance.

READ ME

Follow these steps to increase your chances of success

  • Familiarize yourself with the CBT environment before your exam day.
  • Arrive early at your JAMB center — check your venue and seat number on your exam slip.
  • Bring only the JAMB exam slip and a valid means of identification.
  • Stay calm and focused throughout the test. Avoid rushing or panicking.
  • Listen attentively to all invigilator instructions before starting.
  • If you are unsure about a question, skip it and return later using the navigation panel.
  • Make sure you submit your test before time runs out.
  • After submission, wait for the confirmation screen before leaving your seat.
  • Practice regularly on the Adrena CBT app/website to improve your accuracy and speed.
  • Remember: Success in JAMB depends on understanding, not guesswork. Study smart!
Consistent practice with Adrena JAMB CBT will help you achieve excellence and secure admission in one sitting.
