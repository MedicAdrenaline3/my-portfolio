UI POST-UTME Instructions

Kindly adhere to the following instructions carefully

  • Candidates must score a minimum of 200 in JAMB and possess at least 5 O’level credits at not more than two sittings.
  • UI Post-UTME is a Computer-Based Test (CBT) covering subjects related to your course.
  • Only applicants who complete the online screening form will be considered for admission.
  • Mobile phones and electronic devices are prohibited at the exam venue.
  • Ensure you upload a clear passport photo and accurate bio-data.
  • Biometric verification is compulsory at the exam venue.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled exam time.
  • Dressing must be smart and decent.
  • Print and bring your exam slip and a valid ID to the center.
  • All applicants are advised to frequently check their emails and the university portal for updates.
Post-UTME is calculated as: UTME (50%) + Post-UTME score (50%). Your overall aggregate determines your admission chance.

Follow these steps to increase your chances of success

  • Arrive early at your Post-JAMB exam center. Locate the venue a day before to avoid confusion.
  • Bring only necessary items — avoid mobile phones and banned gadgets.
  • Take extra stationery like pencils, erasers, and calculators.
  • Dress comfortably and smartly to make a good impression.
  • Stay silent in the exam hall. Any noise or misconduct may lead to disqualification.
  • Listen to all instructions and tick or shade correctly. Stop writing once time is up.
  • If unsure about a question, skip it and return later to save time.
  • Don’t rely on luck — consistent practice is key to passing.
  • General Paper is common across schools, so prepare adequately.
This guide and practice will help you pass your Post-JAMB in one sitting.
